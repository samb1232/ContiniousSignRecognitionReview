# A Critical Review on Video-Based Continuous Sign Language Recognition

A video-based CSLR review covering visual feature extraction, temporal modelling, datasets, evaluation and limitations of deep-learning methods.

**Proposed eligible review (full text).** Meets M1–M3 as a dedicated CSLR review. Table 2 compares multiple continuous-recognition architectures, datasets, reported WER and trade-offs, including multi-stream CNN–LSTM–HMM and staged-optimization approaches. [Evidence](../evidence/W4415763805.json).

02 · Eligibility assessment

Screening criteria

Apply M1–M3 to each candidate. AI assessments are proposals; manual decisions are recorded independently.

M1

Review purpose

Include narrative reviews, surveys, systematic reviews, scoping reviews and mapping studies synthesizing previous research. Exclude primary method, dataset or experimental papers whose literature discussion is only background.

M2

CSLR coverage

Include reviews of recognition of sequences of linguistic signs in continuous signing. Broader SLR/translation surveys may qualify.

M3

Vision-based scope

Include video/RGB, depth and pose/skeleton approaches. Exclude sensor-only reviews without vision-based CSLR coverage.

Verdicts and supporting evidence
